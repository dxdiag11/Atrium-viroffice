# Atrium — Cave Recreation Wing

Gunakan prompt di bawah bersama gambar map gua existing sebagai lampiran referensi. Gambar tersebut adalah acuan gaya, material, skala furnitur, dan kesinambungan arsitektur; hasilnya harus berupa map kedua dengan layout baru.

## Ready-to-use image prompt

Create ONE original, premium, polished hand-painted 2D game background map for the recreation and rest wing of an existing subterranean virtual office called Atrium. Do not render the name or any text in the image.

REFERENCE ROLE AND VISUAL CONTINUITY
Use the attached existing cave-office map as the authoritative visual reference. This new map must look like another connected wing of the SAME cave complex, illustrated by the SAME artist for the SAME game. Preserve its visual language: warm sculpted limestone and sandstone boundaries, charcoal/slate stone floors, warm timber furniture, olive and moss-green upholstery, amber lamps, contained tropical greenery, occasional turquoise water and restrained natural crystal accents. Match the reference's line weight, painted surface detail, furniture proportions, soft contact shadows, and overall visual density.

Create an original recreation-focused floor plan, not a duplicate of the office. Do not repeat its office desk clusters, boardroom, reception desk, or server room. Keep the modern, cozy, refined workplace atmosphere. The recreation wing should feel welcoming, social, comfortable, well maintained, and architecturally believable. The party chamber is part of this same tasteful cave campus, not a different visual genre.

CAMERA, CANVAS, AND OUTPUT
- Strictly top-down orthographic, camera looking straight down at 90 degrees. Flat bird's-eye game-map view, no perspective convergence, no tilted camera, no isometric projection.
- Render furniture and fixtures from directly above. Prioritize this camera requirement even if the reference has slightly dimensional details.
- One complete single-floor map on a wide landscape canvas with EXACT 10:7 pixel aspect ratio. Target 3000 × 2100 pixels, or another exact 10:7 resolution supported by the generator. High-resolution PNG output.
- Keep comparable furniture scale relative to the full canvas as the reference; provide spacious chambers by allocating floor area, not by shrinking chairs or beds into tiny symbols.
- All rooms, furniture, cave boundaries, and the connecting threshold must be fully visible. Only the intentional travel passage may meet the top edge. No accidental cropped rooms or furniture.
- Fill the frame with the cave complex and its integrated perimeter rock. No blank outer border, detached floor-plan islands, or large unused expanses of solid rock.
- Remove ceilings and overhead obstructions so all interiors are visible. Cave boundaries should read as solid, non-walkable rock without concealing furniture or entrances.
- Deliver only the flat background artwork. No alternate views, inset diagrams, legend, UI, text, or labels.

SPATIAL CONNECTION TO THE EXISTING MAP — ESSENTIAL
The existing office map has a broad exit passage at its BOTTOM CENTER, directly below its reception area. This second map continues beyond that exit. Therefore, place the new map's ONLY inter-map entrance/exit at the TOP CENTER.

At the top-center edge, create a broad, unmistakable stone passage framed by warm natural rock, matching slate paving, paired amber lamps, and restrained greenery. Its centerline must align approximately with the horizontal midpoint of the canvas. It should visually connect to the bottom-center passage in the reference. Treat any shallow threshold detail as a flat floor transition, not a staircase to a different story.

The passage leads downward into a generous unobstructed arrival vestibule and then into the central social area. Keep the first several character-lengths beyond the threshold free of furniture, plants, raised decorations, railings, and water so several players can spawn and move apart. Maintain a clear direct route back to the same top-center opening. This opening serves both arrival and return travel; do not add a second travel portal or another exterior exit.

Use paving and architectural framing to make the threshold recognizable. Do not draw arrows, glowing teleport circles, spawn markers, coordinate grids, written signs, or interface indicators.

OVERALL FLOOR PLAN
Organize the map as a connected recreation wing with four functional zones plus the arrival vestibule:
- TOP CENTER: shared entrance/exit and open arrival vestibule.
- CENTER / CENTER LEFT: generous communal conversation and rest lounge, the main circulation hub.
- UPPER LEFT, away from the party chamber: a quiet barracks-inspired sleeping room.
- LOWER LEFT or another quiet side alcove: two compact individual bathrooms opening onto a shared accessible passage.
- RIGHT HALF: the largest dedicated chamber, a cave party room with a DJ station, an open dance floor, and peripheral club/bar seating.

Use this arrangement to keep quiet rest spaces away from the party zone. Connect each room through a broad, clearly visible opening from the shared circulation area. Bathrooms must be accessible without crossing the sleeping room or dance floor. Do not create a labyrinth or force players to walk through furniture groups. Thick sculpted cave partitions can suggest acoustic separation between the party chamber and quiet areas. Keep the social hub visibly connected to the party entrance.

ZONE 1 — COMMUNAL CONVERSATION AND REST LOUNGE
Create a comfortable gathering room that can host many players in several small conversational groups.
- Include THREE generously separated round wooden tables, each surrounded by SIX individual chairs, for EIGHTEEN clearly visible table seats total.
- Chairs should be appropriately sized relative to the tables, each oriented toward its table center, with visible floor gaps between neighboring chairs and no chair tucked invisibly under a table.
- Leave broad walking routes around and between all three seating groups, including approach space behind chairs.
- Add a modest secondary sofa nook with two small sofas or a sofa and separate armchairs, one low coffee table, and a muted olive rug, placed outside the main circulation route.
- Use warm wood, olive upholstery, amber lamps, bookshelves against cave boundaries, and contained fern/moss planters.
- A small planted limestone feature may serve as a landmark at the edge of the lounge, but must not obstruct the arrival axis or divide the room into narrow paths.
- Keep table decorations minimal so the seating layout remains instantly readable.

ZONE 2 — TWO SMALL INDIVIDUAL BATHROOMS
Create TWO separate compact bathroom alcoves, each with its own clear doorway opening onto a shared passage.
- Each room contains exactly ONE modern toilet and ONE small wall-side hand basin.
- Show every toilet directly from above with its lid raised or absent and the seat clearly visible as a distinct usable fixture, suitable for later character-sitting interaction.

ZONE 3 — QUIET BARRACKS-INSPIRED REST ROOM
Create an orderly communal sleeping chamber inspired by barracks organization, interpreted as a cozy modern workplace rest area.
- Include SIX single-level beds in TWO orderly rows of THREE, with a broad central aisle and clear access to each bed.
- Use individual single beds, not bunk beds, so every mattress remains fully visible from above and no stacked furniture hides interactive surfaces.

- Add a restrained row of personal lockers or storage cabinets against a cave boundary, plus a few compact bedside lamps or shelves.

Party atmosphere:
- Suggest music and celebration through the DJ equipment, dance-floor layout, and soft accent lighting while maintaining a bright, readable map.

ENVIRONMENT AND MATERIALS
- Cave rock should shape believable connected rooms, with warm stratified texture and intentional sculpted boundaries rather than scattered random boulders.
- Place moss, ferns, and leafy plants in perimeter pockets, small planters, and non-walkable niches. Never conceal seats, bed surfaces, doors, or walking routes with foliage.

GAMEPLAY READABILITY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Every chair, stool, toilet seat, and bed must be individually identifiable and fully visible from above.
- Orient all seats toward their associated table, counter, or conversational group.
- Keep the walking surface distinguishable from solid cave walls, furniture, planting beds, and any water.
- Main circulation should accommodate at least three player avatars side by side at the reference's character-to-furniture scale; room entrances should comfortably allow two players to pass.
- Provide sensible approach space to seats, toilets, beds, bar stools, and the DJ console.

- Use simple, readable furniture footprints that can later be mapped to collision rectangles and floor polygons.
- Keep all functional zones connected to the top-center arrival area without crossing obstacles.
- Decorative detail must never compromise clear navigation.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS
No people, characters, avatars, DJ person, animals, text, labels, room names, logos, brand names, watermarks, UI, arrows, coordinates, navigation overlays, or teleport effects. No isometric or tilted perspective, 3D render, pixel art, horror, danger, abandoned mine aesthetic, monsters, skulls, weapons, fantasy dungeon props, oppressive darkness, fog, or obscuring visual effects. No office workstation clusters or unrelated extra rooms. No copied layout from the reference.

FINAL VISUAL CHECK
The result must immediately read as the recreation wing of the attached cave office: one clear TOP-CENTER connecting passage, a welcoming multi-table conversation lounge, two usable private toilet rooms, an orderly six-bed rest chamber, and a large refined party chamber with one accessible DJ booth facing an empty dance floor and peripheral bar/lounge seating. All zones are fully visible, visually coherent, and navigable in one premium flat top-down game background.

## Catatan implementasi — bukan bagian gambar

- Prompt ini membuat artwork saja. Trigger perpindahan map, spawn, collision, interaksi duduk, dan musik perlu dibuat di kode setelah koordinat gambar final tersedia.
- Jalur yang dimaksud: keluar tengah bawah map existing → muncul di area lapang setelah pintu tengah atas map kedua; kembali melalui pintu yang sama.
- Spawn sebaiknya berada di luar area trigger balik agar pemain tidak langsung berpindah lagi. Gunakan mekanisme keluar-dari-trigger atau cooldown perpindahan.
- Walkie-talkie global tetap harus menjangkau pemain di kedua map; proximity voice normal perlu dibatasi pada pemain di map yang sama. Ini aturan aplikasi, bukan elemen yang perlu divisualisasikan.
- Validasi ukuran PNG setelah generasi. Target 3000 × 2100 mempertahankan rasio tepat 10:7; jumlah pixel dua kali lebih besar tidak berarti furnitur dua kali lebih besar di world game. Tentukan ukuran world dan pemetaan koordinat secara konsisten saat implementasi.
